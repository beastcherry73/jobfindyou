# Veo / Kling prompts — JobSpike photoreal cinematic clips

Paste these into Google AI Studio (Veo) or Kling, one at a time. Character
bible is repeated verbatim in every prompt so the model renders a consistent
person. Generate at 5-8s each. Once downloaded, name the files clip1.mp4
through clip5.mp4 (in story order) and drop them in
`marketing/nas-daily-video/cinematic/clips_in/`, then run
`assemble_cinematic.py`.

Craft note: none of these prompts ask the model to render legible on-screen
UI/text (score numbers, resume text) -- Veo/Kling hallucinate garbled text
almost every time that's attempted. The laptop/phone screens stay soft glow
only; the real score UI gets composited from an actual jobspike.in screen
recording later, which is also more credible for the "20 seconds, 98%" beats.

## PROMPT 1 -- Discouraged, rejection emails (~6s)

A 26-year-old South Asian man with short neat black hair, light stubble,
warm brown skin, wearing a heather-gray crewneck sweater over a white
t-shirt, sits at a small wooden desk in a tidy home study at dusk. A warm
brass desk lamp casts soft amber key light on his face; a window behind him
shows dim blue evening light; a blurred bookshelf in the background.
Photorealistic, cinematic 35mm lens look, shallow depth of field, natural
skin texture, subtle film grain, warm color grade.

Action: He holds his phone up in front of his face, thumb slowly scrolling.
His expression sinks -- brow furrows, shoulders drop, he exhales and lowers
the phone slightly, staring past it. The phone screen glows softly on his
face but no readable text is visible, just a wash of light with small red
dots suggesting notifications.

Camera: slow static push-in, barely perceptible, centered on his face and
upper body.
Duration: 6 seconds. No dialogue, no on-screen text, no cartoon or
illustrated style, no distorted hands.
