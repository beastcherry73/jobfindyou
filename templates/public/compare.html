Where ResuMax is ahead

  • A curated project library, reviewed by them, rather than projects generated for your gaps.
  • An MCP integration, so their agent can be used from ChatGPT or Claude.
  • Depth in tech hiring specifically — it is built for software, PM, design and ML roles and nothing else.
  • A longer track record: they launched in October 2025 and publish usage numbers; JobSpike is newer and smaller.

Where JobSpike is ahead

  • Price: the features above are free, with no monthly caps.
  • Every listing comes from an employer's own careers page, so applying never routes through an aggregator repost.
  • Salary figures come from published ranges in live postings, with the sample size shown and no estimate when the sample is too small.
  • Answer scoring quotes your own words back, and discards any feedback that quotes something you did not write.

How we keep this fair
