# 02_FEATURE_ROADMAP: Future Milestones & Current Capabilities

## Current Scope (V1.0)
*   **Arctic Horizon Homepage**: 14 sections, parallax hero elements, upload portals, and Google/Email auth gate.
*   **AI Optimizer (Workspace)**: Experience builder, resume preview, and PDF compilation.

## Upcoming Scope (V1.1 - V2.0)
1.  **Stateless Sandboxed Analysis**: Evaluate parsed resume PDF upload payloads dynamically, showing raw score trends prior to sign-up.
2.  **Persistent SQLite / PostgreSQL Data Storage**: Enable saving multiple resume drafts per user, matching them against various target job descriptions.
3.  **Dynamic Interview Prep Simulator**: Interactive chatbot asking specialized technical questions targeting identified keyword gaps.
4.  **Interactive Skill Roadmaps**: Step-by-step master checklists for frontend/backend/DevOps tracks with progress state saves.
5.  **Application Pipeline Kanban**: Drag-and-drop boards to track candidates' statuses through Saved, Applied, Interview, and Offer pipelines.
