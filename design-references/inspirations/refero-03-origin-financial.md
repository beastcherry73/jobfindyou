# Refero pick 3: Origin Financial

**Source:** styles.refero.design · **Type:** approachable-serious product reference
**Status:** KEEP — for goals, progress, and "serious but human."

**Why it fits JobSpike:** Origin is a personal-finance app that stays warm and
encouraging while handling weighty, anxiety-adjacent content (your money / your
future). JobSpike handles the career equivalent (your job search / your worth). It
models the exact tone we want: serious tool, human warmth, not intimidating.

**Borrow:** progress/goal visualizations, encouraging-but-honest copy, milestone
framing, warm neutral palette with a single confident accent.

**Avoid:** anything that tips into "life-coach/wellness" fluff — stay product-focused.

**Applies to:** Career Workspace (goals, progress), coach recommendations, empty states.
