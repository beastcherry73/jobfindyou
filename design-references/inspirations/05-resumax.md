# Inspiration: resumax.ai

**Type:** live site (competitor — career product)
**Status:** SCOPE REFERENCE ONLY — not a visual one.

**What it is:** A comprehensive career product: resume scoring, AI bullet rewrite,
job matching (700+ companies, daily digest, match scores), mock interviews, cover
letters, project library, MCP integrations, salary negotiation. Card-based,
data-driven, professional SaaS look.

**Borrow:** as a FEATURE checklist to compare against — cover-letter generation is
the cheapest high-value add.

**Avoid:** letting its scope creep into JobSpike. Each of its features (job
matching, interview prep) is its own product decision with backend cost, not a
screen to copy. Its visual style is generic; JobSpike's identity is stronger.
