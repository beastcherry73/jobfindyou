# Inspiration: madewithgsap.com

**Type:** live site (GSAP animation marketplace)
**Status:** CONFIRMS existing approach — little new.

**What it is:** A curated animation-effects library. Minimalist, generous
whitespace, bold type, monochrome, clear information hierarchy. Its funnel:
emotional hook → social proof → features → pricing.

**Borrow:** confirmation that JobSpike's marketing funnel order is already sound
(progressive disclosure of value). No structural change needed.

**Avoid:** treating "made with GSAP" as a reason to add heavy animation; the
lesson here is the clean hierarchy, not the effects.
