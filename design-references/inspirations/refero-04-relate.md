# Refero pick 4: Relate (CRM)

**Source:** styles.refero.design · **Type:** pipeline/CRM reference
**Status:** KEEP — directly maps to the Applications tracker.

**Why it fits JobSpike:** A CRM's core job — moving records through stages
(lead → deal) and tracking history — is structurally identical to a job search
(saved → applied → interview → offer). Best real-world reference for our
Applications surface.

**Borrow:** pipeline/stage layout, per-record status at a glance, activity/event
history on a record, calm list+detail patterns. Pairs with our "stage spine" +
card-expand-to-timeline signature patterns (see 03-signature-patterns.md).

**Avoid:** enterprise-CRM density/clutter; keep it light for an individual user.

**Applies to:** Applications tracker.
